# Lumina for parents: a simple guide

How to see your children's learning, download a report, set learning-time controls, and talk with your
children's teachers. (Developers: the
technical side is in the README, "Parent experience".)

To try it with the demo account: go to `/sign-in`, email **parent@lumina.test**, password
**lumina-demo-password**. Leo has learning to look at; Maya hasn't started yet.

---

## 1. Your dashboard: all your children at a glance

Sign in. You land on **Overview** (it's also in the menu). There is one card for each child:

| On the card | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Grade** and **Active today / 3 days ago** | Their grade, and when they last learned something |
| **Last 7 days** | Time spent in lessons this week |
| **Streak** | Days in a row with learning |
| **Lessons done** | Lessons finished, since the start |
| **Courses** | Courses going, and courses finished |
| **Latest quiz** | Their most recent quiz and its score |
| **School work** | Work from their teacher that is **overdue** (red) or **due this week** (yellow) |
| **Controls on** | Shown when you set a time limit or bedtime |

Press **See (name)'s progress** to open that child's page.

No card yet? Press **Manage children** to add your child, or to link the account they already have at school.

## 2. A child's page: progress, grades and activity

The page has two tabs at the top: **Progress** and **Parental controls**.

On **Progress**, from top to bottom:

1. **The numbers**: time in lessons (last 14 days), streak, lessons finished, **quiz average**, courses
   finished, level, badges, and all learning time.
2. **Doing well** (green) and **Worth practising** (yellow). Simple rules you can check yourself:
   - Doing well: a course finished, a quiz scored 80% or more, 3 or more days of learning in a row.
   - Worth practising: a quiz under 60%, review questions missed more than once, school work that is overdue.
3. **Courses**: each course with a progress bar (for example "3 of 4 lessons · 75%") and the next lesson.
4. **Quiz results**: the latest try of each quiz, with the score and the date. Written answers aren't marked
   by the computer, so they are listed but not counted in the score.
5. **Work set by teachers**: what the teacher set, when it is due, and whether it is done.
6. **Learning time**: a bar for each of the last 14 days.
7. **Recent activity**: lessons finished, quizzes finished, badges and certificates, newest first, with the
   date and time.
8. **Badges and certificates**, and the **parental controls** that are on.

You see the same numbers your child sees on their own home page. Your child's notes and quiz answers are not
shown here (they are in **Download their data** on **My children**, with everything else).

## 3. Download a learning report (PDF)

At the top of a child's page:

1. Under **Report period**, choose **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days**.
2. Press **Download report (PDF)**.

The report has the period's learning time, days with learning, lessons and quizzes finished; doing well and
worth practising; courses, quiz results and school work; learning time per day or week; badges and certificates
earned; and the parental controls. Print it, keep it, or share it with a teacher.

The file name has the date, not your child's name (for example
`lumina-learning-report-2026-11-16-30-days.pdf`).

## 4. Parental controls: learning time and bedtime

Open a child's page, then the **Parental controls** tab.

1. **Daily learning time**: choose a limit (15 minutes to 2 hours), or **No limit**. It counts time spent in
   lessons and starts again every day.
2. **Bedtime**: tick **Set a bedtime**, then choose **From** and **Until** (for example 20:00 to 07:00). It
   uses the clock on the device your child learns on.
3. **When the time is up**, choose:
   - **A reminder to take a break**: a message appears, and your child can carry on.
   - **Lessons and quizzes pause**: lessons, quizzes and review show a "Time for a break" or "It's bedtime"
     screen until the next day, or until bedtime ends. The rest of Lumina (their home page, badges) stays open.
4. Check the box **What (name) gets**: it says in plain words what will happen.
5. Press **Save controls**.

Good to know:

- **Nothing is lost.** Lessons and quiz answers are saved as your child goes, before a break screen appears.
- **Your child is told why.** The message says the limit or bedtime was chosen by their parent.
- **Shared by both parents.** Every parent or guardian linked to the child sees and can change the same
  controls. Under the Save button you see who changed them last, and when.
- To turn everything off: choose **No limit**, untick **Set a bedtime**, and press **Save controls**.
- These controls are a friendly reminder system inside Lumina, not a lock for the whole device.

## 5. Messages: talk with your child's teacher

Open **Messages** in the menu. A number next to it means something new arrived.

**Start a conversation**

1. Press **New message**.
2. Choose your **child**, then the **teacher** (you see the teachers of your child's classes).
3. Press **Open conversation**.

**Write and read**

- Type in **Write a message** and press **Send** (or **Ctrl + Enter** on a keyboard).
- New messages appear **straight away**, without reloading the page. At the top you see
  **Live: new messages appear straight away**. (If your connection can't stay live, it says
  **Checking for new messages every 20 seconds** instead, and still works.)
- Beside the messages (below them on a phone) you see **your child's work with that teacher**: what is done,
  in progress or not started, and when it is due. You both talk about the same thing.
- If a message doesn't send, your text **stays in the box**. Try again.

**Share a progress update**

Press **Share a progress update** to send a card with your child's **learning at home** in the last 7 days
(time in lessons, days with learning, lessons finished, streak, latest quiz, courses in progress). You can add a
note. Nothing is shared unless you press the button. The teacher can share a card too: your child's work they
set, and how far along it is.

**Good to know**

- One conversation per teacher and child. If both parents are on Lumina, you are both in it.
- Your child never sees these messages.
- If your child moves to another class, the conversation with the old teacher closes.

## 6. Announcements from the class

Open **Messages**, then the **Announcements** tab. This is where your child's teacher writes to the whole class:
trips, reminders, news. New ones are marked **New** and arrive straight away. Press **Mark as read** when
you've read one (the teacher sees how many parents have read it).

## 7. Privacy

- You only ever see your own children. Another family's child can't be opened, even with the link.
- Teachers can't see your child's progress page. In a conversation, a teacher sees your child's work they set,
  and what you choose to share.
- **My children → Download their data** gives you everything Lumina stores about your child, including the
  controls you set and your messages with teachers. Deleting a child's account deletes all of it.
