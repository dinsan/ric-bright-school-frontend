# Lumina for parents: a simple guide

How to see your children's learning, set goals to help them do better, download a report, set learning-time
controls, and set up picture sign-in for young children. (Developers: the technical side is in
[`parent-experience.md`](parent-experience.md).)

**Signing in.** Parents have their own sign-in page: on `/sign-in` choose **I'm a parent** (or go straight to
`/sign-in/parent`). Your children sign in on the student page with the username and password (or pictures)
you set up for them; each of you sees your own side of Lumina. If you type your details on the student
page by mistake, Lumina tells you and offers the right page.

To try it with the demo account: go to `/sign-in/parent`, email **parent@lumina.test**, password
**lumina-demo-password**. Leo has learning to look at and three goals; Maya hasn't started yet, and signs in with
pictures (family code **SUNNY7**, then cat, sun, tree).

**Need help?** Press the **?** button at the top of any page (or the **?** key). You see the help for the page
you're on first, and you can search ("goal", "report", "bedtime"). Each answer has step-by-step instructions and a
button that opens the right page, for the child you're looking at. Everything is also on one page: press your
initials at the top right, then **Help** (`/help/parents`). Your children have their own, simpler help in their
panel.

Not signed up yet? The same **?** button is on the home page and the sign-in pages, and the **help centre**
(`/help`, also at the bottom of every public page) explains what Lumina is, what it costs, how to create your
account and what you and your children can do, before you sign up.

## 1. Your dashboard: all your children at a glance

Sign in. You land on **Overview** (it's also in the menu). There is one card for each child:

| On the card | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Grade** and **Active today / 3 days ago** | Their grade, and when they last learned something |
| **Last 7 days** | Time spent in lessons this week |
| **Streak** | Days in a row with learning |
| **Lessons done** | Lessons finished, since the start |
| **Courses** | Courses going, and courses finished |
| **Latest quiz** | Their most recent quiz and its score |
| **Goals** | How the goals you set are going: **met**, **on track**, **behind** or **missed** |
| **Controls on** | Shown when you set a time limit or bedtime |

Press **See (name)'s progress** to open that child's page.

No card yet? Press **Manage children** to add your child, or to link the Lumina account they already have.

## 2. A child's page: progress, grades and activity

The page has three tabs at the top: **Progress**, **Goals** and **Parental controls**.

On **Progress**, from top to bottom:

1. **The numbers**: time in lessons (last 14 days), streak, lessons finished, **quiz average**, courses
   finished, level, badges, and all learning time.
2. **Doing well** (green) and **Worth practising** (yellow). Simple rules you can check yourself:
   - Doing well: a course finished, a quiz scored 80% or more, 3 or more days of learning in a row, a goal met.
   - Worth practising: a quiz under 60%, review questions missed more than once, a goal that is behind or missed.
3. **Courses**: each course with a progress bar (for example "3 of 4 lessons · 75%") and the next lesson.
4. **Quiz results**: the latest try of each quiz, with the score and the date. Written answers aren't scored,
   so they are listed but not counted in the score.
5. **Goals**: each goal you set, how far along your child is, and the past weeks.
6. **Learning time**: a bar for each of the last 14 days.
7. **Recent activity**: lessons finished, quizzes finished, goals met, badges and certificates, newest first, with the
   date and time.
8. **Badges and certificates**, and the **parental controls** that are on.

You see the same numbers your child sees on their own home page. Your child's notes and quiz answers are not
shown here (they are in **Download their data** on **My children**, with everything else).

## 3. Download a learning report (PDF)

At the top of a child's page:

1. Under **Report period**, choose **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days**.
2. Press **Download report (PDF)**.

The report has the period's learning time, days with learning, lessons and quizzes finished; doing well and
worth practising; courses, quiz results and goals; learning time per day or week; badges and certificates
earned; and the parental controls. Print it, or keep it to see progress over time.

The file name has the date, not your child's name (for example
`lumina-learning-report-2026-11-16-30-days.pdf`).

## 4. Parental controls: learning time and bedtime

Open a child's page, then the **Parental controls** tab.

1. **Daily learning time**: choose a limit (15 minutes to 2 hours), or **No limit**. It counts time spent in
   lessons and starts again every day.
2. **Bedtime**: tick **Set a bedtime**, then choose **From** and **Until** (for example 20:00 to 07:00). It
   uses the clock on the device your child learns on.
3. **When the time is up**, choose:
   - **A reminder to take a break**: a message appears, and your child can carry on.
   - **Lessons and quizzes pause**: lessons, quizzes and review show a "Time for a break" or "It's bedtime"
     screen until the next day, or until bedtime ends. The rest of Lumina (their home page, badges) stays open.
4. Check the box **What (name) gets**: it says in plain words what will happen.
5. Press **Save controls**.

Good to know:

- **Nothing is lost.** Lessons and quiz answers are saved as your child goes, before a break screen appears.
- **Your child is told why.** The message says the limit or bedtime was chosen by their parent.
- **Shared by both parents.** Every parent or guardian linked to the child sees and can change the same
  controls. Under the Save button you see who changed them last, and when.
- To turn everything off: choose **No limit**, untick **Set a bedtime**, and press **Save controls**.
- These controls are a friendly reminder system inside Lumina, not a lock for the whole device.

## 5. Goals: help your child aim for something clear

Open a child's page, then the **Goals** tab. A child can have up to **5 goals** at a time: a few clear goals work
better than many.

**Set a goal**

1. Under **Set a new goal**, choose **what kind of goal**:
   - **Time each week**: minutes of learning from Monday to Sunday (for example 1 hour).
   - **Days each week**: learn on so many days a week (a lesson, a quiz or a review all count).
   - **Lessons each week**: finish so many lessons from Monday to Sunday.
   - **Finish a course**: every lesson of a course your child has started, by a day you choose.
   - **A quiz score**: reach a score in a quiz (only tries from now on count), with a day if you like.
2. Choose the **target**, and the **course** or **quiz** when the kind needs one.
3. Add **a note for your child** if you like: a few words of encouragement. They see it with the goal.
4. Read **(name) will see: …** to check the goal says what you mean, then press **Set the goal**.

**Follow it**

- Each goal shows how far along your child is ("40 of 60 minutes this week"), what's left ("20 more minutes by
  Sunday"), and whether it is **on track**, **behind**, **met** or **missed**. Weekly goals show the past weeks too.
- Your child sees exactly the same goals and numbers on their home page, and Lumina celebrates when they meet one.
- Goals count in your child's own week, Monday to Sunday, and start again every Monday.
- **Pause** a goal to hide it from your child for a while (it stops counting); **Resume** brings it back.
  **Remove** deletes it, with the weeks it was met (Lumina asks first).
- Every parent or guardian linked to the child sees and can change the same goals.

## 6. Picture sign-in for young children: the family code

Children in Grades 1 to 3 usually find pictures easier than a password.

1. On **My children**, press **Change sign-in** (or **Set up sign-in**) for your child, choose **Pictures**, a
   username and three pictures in order, and save.
2. Under **Family code for picture sign-in**, press **Make a family code**. You get a 6-character code.
3. On your child's device, open **Sign in with pictures** (from the student sign-in page), type the family code,
   and tick **This is our family's shared device** if the device stays at home: next time your child only picks
   their name and pictures.
4. Your child presses their name, then their three pictures.

- Only your children who sign in with pictures appear after the code.
- Think someone else has the code? Press **Make a new code**: the old one stops working straight away.
- A picture sign-in on a shared device ends after 8 hours, so the next child doesn't land in the wrong account.

## 7. Privacy

- You only ever see your own children. Another family's child can't be opened, even with the link.
- Nobody on Lumina can message your child, and your child can't message anyone. The only words your child reads
  from another person are the notes you add to their goals.
- **My children → Download their data** gives you everything Lumina stores about your child, including their
  goals and the controls you set. Deleting a child's account deletes all of it.
