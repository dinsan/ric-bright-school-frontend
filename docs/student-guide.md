# Lumina for students: a simple guide

How to sign in, follow your goals, and use notes and bookmarks, quizzes, streaks, points, badges and certificates.
Written for students, and for the parents who help them. (Developers: the technical side is in [`student-experience.md`](student-experience.md).)

**Signing in.** Students have their own sign-in page: on `/sign-in` choose **I'm a student** (or go
straight to `/sign-in/student`) and type the username and password your parent set up for you. Do you use
pictures? Press **Sign in with pictures**, type your family's code (your parent has it), press your name, then
your three pictures in the right order. Your parent signs in on a different page and sees how you are doing, not
your lessons.

To try everything with the demo account: go to `/sign-in/student`, username **leo10**, password
**lumina-demo-password**.

**Need help?** Press the **?** button at the top of any page (or the **?** key on a keyboard). Lumina shows help
for the page you're on first, and you can type what you need ("quiz", "notes", "streak"). Each answer tells you
step by step what to press, and its button takes you straight there. All the help is on one page too: press your
initial at the top right, then **Help** (`/help/students`). Can't sign in? The **?** button is on the sign-in
pages too.

## Your goals

Your parent can set **goals** to help you do your best, like "Learn 1 hour a week", "Learn on 3 days a week" or
"Finish Fractions Made Simple by Friday". You find them on your **Home** page and under **Goals** in the menu.

- Each goal shows how far along you are ("40 of 60 minutes this week") and what's left ("20 more minutes by
  Sunday"). It says **On track**, **Behind**, **Met** or **Missed**.
- Your parent can add a little note to a goal. You see it with the goal.
- Weekly goals start again every **Monday**. The past weeks show whether you met them.
- When you meet a goal, Lumina says **Goal met!**
- Press the button on a goal (**Keep learning**, **Open the course**, **Take the quiz**) to work on it.

## 1. Notes: write down what you want to remember

You can write notes inside any lesson of a course you have started.

1. Open a lesson.
2. At the top of the screen, press the **notebook** button (it shows how many notes you have in this lesson).
3. A panel opens on the side. In **New note**, type what you want to remember.
4. Under **About**, choose what your note is about: **The whole lesson**, or one part of it (for example
   "Video: What is a fraction?").
5. Press **Save note** (or **Ctrl + Enter** on a keyboard).

Later you can press **Edit** to change a note, or **Delete** to remove it (Lumina asks "Delete this note?"
first, so you can't delete one by accident). If a note is about a part of the lesson, press the part's name
above the note to jump straight to it.

- If your internet drops while saving, your text **stays in the box**: nothing is lost. Press Save again.
- Your notes are **private**: your teachers and classmates can't see them. (A parent who downloads a copy of
  your account's information gets them too, like everything else about your account.)
- Write 5 notes and you earn the **Note taker** badge.

## 2. Bookmarks: find things again quickly

**Bookmark a whole lesson**: at the top of the lesson, press the **bookmark** (ribbon) button. It fills in
when the lesson is bookmarked. Press it again to remove the bookmark.

**Bookmark a moment in a video**: under the video, press **Bookmark this moment (0:42)**. The time appears
under the video as a small button: press it any time to jump back to that moment.

**See a lesson's bookmarks**: open the notebook panel and choose the **Bookmarks** tab. **Go there** takes you
to the part of the lesson, and starts the video at the bookmarked moment.

## 3. All your notes and bookmarks in one place

Go to **My courses**, then the **Notes and bookmarks** tab (or press **My notes** on your home page).

- **Bookmarks** come first. Press **Open** to go back to the exact place, even the exact moment of a video.
- **Notes** are grouped by course and lesson, newest first.
- Use **Search** to find a note by a word in it, or by the lesson's name.
- The bin button deletes a note or removes a bookmark.

## 4. Points, levels, streaks and badges

Your home page (**Home** in the menu) shows how you are doing:

| What | How you get it |
| --- | --- |
| **XP (points)** | 10 XP for every lesson you finish, 50 XP for every course you finish |
| **Level** | Level 2 at 100 XP; each next level needs 50 XP more than the last. The bar shows how close you are |
| **Streak** (the flame) | Learn something every day: open or finish a lesson, do your review, or finish a quiz. Today still counts until the day ends |
| **Badges** | 10 badges, such as **First step** (finish your first lesson), **On a roll** (3 days in a row), **Course champion** (finish a course), **Note taker** (write 5 notes) |

- See every badge in **Achievements → Badges**. Badges you haven't earned yet are grey with a lock, and each one
  says how to earn it.
- When you earn a badge or reach a new level, a message pops up. Finishing a course shows a big "You finished!"
  message with your certificate.
- Don't like the animations? On your home page, turn **Celebrations** off. You still get the messages, just
  without the bouncing.
- There are no leaderboards: you never compete with other children.

## 5. Certificates: download, print, keep

You get a certificate **automatically** when you finish every lesson of a course.

1. Go to **Achievements → Certificates** (or press **See my certificate** in the "You finished!" message).
2. Press **Preview** to see it big. On a phone, slide it sideways to see all of it, or press **Open PDF** to
   zoom in.
3. Press **Download PDF** to save it, then print it or send it to someone.

Each certificate has your name, the course, the date, and a number like **LUM-7K2Q-9XHD**. If you leave a
course later, you keep its certificate. Certificates are private: only you can see them in Lumina.

## 6. Quizzes: show what you know

Some courses have quizzes. You find them on your course's page (**My courses**, then the course), under
**Quizzes**. Start the course first; then press a quiz to open it.

1. The first screen says how many questions there are, and whether you see them **one at a time** or **all on
   one page** (the course decides). Press **Start the quiz**.
2. Answer the questions. Some ask you to choose one answer, some to choose all the right ones, to type a word or a
   number, to fill in blanks in a sentence, to match things, or to put things in order (use the **up** and
   **down** arrows next to each one). Numbers can be typed like **12**, **2.5** or **3/4**.
3. Not sure? Press **Flag for review**. The question gets a little flag, so you can find it again.
4. The numbered squares at the top (**Questions**) show every question: **filled in** when you answered it,
   **dashed** when you haven't yet, and a **flag** when you flagged it. Press a number to go to that question.
5. When you're ready, press **Finish quiz**. Lumina shows which questions you haven't answered and which you
   flagged, so you can go back to them. Press **Keep working** to go back, or **Finish and submit** when you're sure.
   After that your answers can't be changed.

- Your answers are **saved as you go** ("Saved" next to the numbers). If you close the quiz or your internet
  drops, open it again and carry on where you stopped.
- You see your score straight away. Written answers (where you explain in your own words) aren't marked, so
  they aren't in your score: read them again and check them against the lesson.
- You can take a quiz again: press **Take the quiz again**.

## Time for a break?

Your parent can choose how long you learn each day, and a bedtime. When you reach it, Lumina shows a message.
Sometimes it's just a reminder ("Time for a break?") and you can carry on; sometimes lessons and quizzes take a
break until tomorrow (or until bedtime is over). Don't worry: everything you did is saved.

## For parents

- Your dashboard shows each child's progress, goals, quiz results and activity; you can set goals, download a
  report and set learning-time controls. How: [`parent-guide.md`](parent-guide.md).
- **Download their data** (under **My children**) gives you a copy of everything Lumina stores about your child,
  including their notes, bookmarks, quiz answers and marks, badges, certificates, goals and the controls you set.
- Notes and bookmarks are never shown to other students.
