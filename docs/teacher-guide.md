# Lumina for teachers: talking with families

How to write to your pupils' parents, share progress, and send class announcements. (Developers: the technical
side is in the README, "Parents and teachers talking".)

To try it with the demo account: go to `/sign-in`, email **teacher@lumina.test**, password
**lumina-demo-password**. Leo's parent has already written to you.

Messages and announcements are for **verified** teachers: an admin checks every teacher first. Your dashboard
says when you are verified.

---

## 1. Messages with a pupil's parents

Open **Messages** in the menu. A number next to it means new messages arrived.

**Start a conversation**

1. Press **New message**.
2. Choose the **class**, then the **pupil**. Pupils whose parents aren't on Lumina yet are shown, but can't be
   chosen ("no parent on Lumina yet").
3. Press **Open conversation**.

**Write and read**

- Type in **Write a message** and press **Send** (or **Ctrl + Enter**).
- New messages appear **straight away**, without reloading ("Live" at the top).
- All of the pupil's parents and guardians are in the conversation. The pupil never sees it.
- Beside the messages you see **the pupil's work you set**: done, in progress or not started, and the due date.

**Share a progress update**

Press **Share a progress update** to send a card with the pupil's work you set and how far along it is (overdue
work is highlighted), with an optional note. Parents can share a card about learning at home too.

## 2. Announcements to a class

Open **Announcements** in the menu.

1. Choose the **class**. Lumina says how many of your pupils' parents are on Lumina and will see it.
2. Write a **title** and the **announcement**.
3. Press **Send to parents**. They see it straight away.

Under **Sent** you see each announcement with **Read by 3 of 5 parents**, updated live. **Remove** takes one down
for everyone.

## Good to know

- You only ever see conversations about pupils in your classes. If a pupil leaves your class, the conversation
  closes.
- To keep things calm, Lumina limits how many messages (30 in 10 minutes) and announcements (20 an hour) one
  person can send.
- Messages are plain text.
